  no Send again; attempts 2 still retries.

  rows come back "interrupted by a restart — it may already have arrived";
  the GUI shows "Waiting for your approval — <error>". Nothing re-sends.

  start; a dead tab still fails visibly. Marker 2026-10-22.1, dist rebuilt.
